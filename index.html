📖 Introduction to the Kumon Recommended Reading List

📚 What is the Kumon Recommended Reading List?

The Kumon Recommended Reading List (RRL) is a list of 380 books intended to help Kumon students find books that they will enjoy reading, and to encourage them to read books from a wide range of genres and styles.

📋 How are the Books Chosen?

The books on the RRL have been selected because they are popular, award winning, or recognised literary classics. They were all available from major online bookshops at the time of writing.

📂 How are the Books Arranged?

The books are divided into twelve sections, broadly corresponding to the levels of the Kumon English Programme. Each title includes a short blurb to help students choose books, and students can colour in stars to rate the books.

👶 Read Together

This section was created for children who haven't started reading independently yet.

📖 Level 2A to Level I

The thirty books in each level from Level 2A to Level I are listed in order of difficulty, with the easiest at number one.

🌟 Further Reading

These thirty books are recommended for more confident, mature readers. They include classics and seminal works from a range of genres and periods.

📚 A Wide Range of Books for a Wide Range of Readers!

The RRL contains something for everyone. Not every book will be appropriate for every student, but everyone will find something they will enjoy!

👨‍👩‍👧 Information for Parents

The following information is intended to help parents make the best use of the Recommended Reading List (RRL), and to give ideas on how to help inspire in children a lifelong love of reading.

🏠 Creating a 'Reading-Friendly' Environment at Home

Store your child's books within reach. Create a reading corner. Visit the library together. Take an active interest in what your child is reading.

📚 Recommending Books

Children should read books that match their ability and interests, which may not correspond to their age or school year.

🗣️ Reading Aloud to Your Child

The stories you read aloud are their first introduction to the world of books. Let your child choose, read at their pace, and talk about the story.

🚀 Encouraging Independent Reading

Children feel achievement when reading a whole book. Listen, give encouragement, and start with simple books.

⭐ More Confident Readers

Continue to recommend books. Use genre icons to explore. Don't force reading choices; keep it fun and satisfying.

📖 The Kumon Recommended Reading List helps students discover the joy of reading!
